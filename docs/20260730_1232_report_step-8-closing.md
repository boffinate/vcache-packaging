The fixture never expanded the production VMOD set. Three independent guards keep `step8-fixture` out of a release: `SCOPE.md` still selects three VMODs, `validate --require-releasable` refuses while the fourteen `pending` alias entries exist, and both selftest modules carry NEVER MERGE blocks naming seven ids absent from main. ### Proof A — run 30536439592: the escape-hatch three-family draft

Dispatched from main @ 88cf8dd with `track=release`, `allow_incomplete_evidence=true`. Conclusion success, all jobs green through `assemble-draft-release`. Draft `draft-20260730T111052Z-88cf8dd9b914` was created with `isDraft=true` and 57 assets — every design category present (6 native packages across three families and two targets, debug packages, `.dsc`, `.debian.tar.xz`, source archives, `RELEASE-SHA256SUMS`, `release-manifest.json`, plus the `.changes`/`.buildinfo`/`.metadata`/`.sha256` sidecars). The body contains the "Evidence gaps" / "This draft is explicitly experimental" section, and the manifest's `evidence_gaps` has 10 entries, the first being the expected dict gap verbatim: `registry/targets/vinyl-9.0.1-ac4f719c16f4/el9-x86_64.yml: vmods.dict.tests.upgrade_transactions is 'pending'; a releasable target needs 'pass'`. The draft was deleted after evidence capture; drafts create no git tag, confirmed against `ls-remote`.

Two honest notes. First, the 3e note expected roughly 20 assets; the actual 57 is the sidecar files, a documented-expectation mismatch to reconcile, not a defect. Second, the other 9 gaps are a **newly found defect on unmodified main**: every cachetag/vinyl EL9 audited-recipe artifact fails checksum verification with `bad_checksums … listed but not in the artifact`, because `recipes/el9/container/build.sh:306` writes `/out/SHA256SUMS` with bare filenames while the RPMs land in `/out/packages/`, and the gate (tools/ci_matrix.py:2091) resolves names relative to the checksum file, exactly as `sha256sum -c` would. The audited-recipe EL9 layout can therefore never verify: **main cannot currently produce `complete=true` even once dict's verdict is resolved, and every release-tier draft needs the escape hatch until the layout or build.sh:306 is fixed.** The selftests all pass because their fixture uses a self-consistent layout; only the live dispatch could surface this.
